Speaking and teaching

Talks, demonstrations, and teaching sessions around robotics, hands-on engineering, and applied systems. I use these sessions to connect research ideas with the practical work of building, testing, and explaining real machines.

CIR, IIIT Allahabad

3 photos

Research discussion | Robotics systems | IIIT Allahabad

Technical session at the Centre for Intelligent Robotics, IIIT Allahabad, focused on how robotics research turns into working systems. The discussion connected manipulation, autonomy, hardware integration, and the engineering decisions needed to move from prototype ideas to practical demonstrations.

Robotics research System integration Manipulation Autonomy

Battery Disassembly

2 photos

Research demonstration | Direct recycling | Human-robot collaboration

Research demonstration of a semi-autonomous lithium-ion battery disassembly system for direct recycling. The session walked through the UR5e manipulation workflow, AprilTag-based localization, ROS and MoveIt planning, and the custom mechanism used to secure, cut, and extract pouch-cell electrode stacks.

UR5e ROS and MoveIt AprilTag localization Mechanism design
