A research demonstration of a semi-autonomous lithium-ion battery disassembly system for direct recycling. The session walked through the UR5e manipulation workflow, AprilTag-based localization, ROS and MoveIt planning, and the custom mechanism used to secure, cut, and extract pouch-cell electrode stacks.
